# "Why now" briefs

Run date: 2026-11-11. Sample: 60 of the 200 universe accounts. Gantrywell is a
fictional sample client. Every claim below links to its source and was
re-opened by a second reviewer. Pain points are hypotheses, not facts about
these companies. People are referred to by role only.

Six accounts have a current verified signal. Fifty-four do not, and are not
briefed: 37 have "no reason to call yet" and 17 were removed as parts of larger
groups.

---

## 1. Wixon (A047), food and beverage, St. Francis, WI

- **Score:** 25 (fit check: fit)
- **Signal:** Wixon completed an expansion in the Milwaukee area that, per the
  article, "more than tripled the operation's footprint and created 110 new
  jobs." Published 2026-11-05, six days old.
  Source: [Perishable News](https://perishablenews.com/?p=437595)
- **Likely pain:** A tripled footprint may mean far more equipment and a second
  site to schedule maintenance across, which can strain spreadsheet or paper
  routines.
- **Opening angle:** Congratulate the expansion, then ask how maintenance work
  is being planned across the old and new sites.
- **Contact:** VP of Operations; the Maintenance Manager as champion.
- **Caveat:** The new site is not the St. Francis plant on file.

## 2. Cooper Farms (A010), food and beverage, St. Henry and Van Wert, OH

- **Score:** 25 (fit check: fit)
- **Signal:** Cooper Farms plans a 28,000-square-foot further-processing plant
  in Celina, Ohio. Published 2026-05-21, 174 days old, so it is close to the
  point where its weight halves.
  Source: [The Daily Standard](https://www.dailystandard.com/archive/2026-05-21/stories/54861/cooper-farms-plans-processing-plant-in-celina)
- **Likely pain:** A third site would add equipment to maintain and may prompt
  a review of how work orders are handled across plants.
- **Opening angle:** Ask how maintenance will be set up at the new plant
  before it opens.
- **Contact:** VP or Director of Operations.
- **Caveat:** The company's 2026 maintenance-supervisor job posts were closed
  by the time they were checked, so they are not counted.

## 3. Shearer's Foods, Phoenix plant (A043), food and beverage

  Ohio. Published 2026-08-02, 101 days old.
  Source: [PotatoPro](https://www.potatopro.com/node/173705)
